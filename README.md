## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a **Power BI RFM Customer Segmentation Dashboard** designed to analyze customer purchasing behavior and identify meaningful customer segments.

The analysis uses **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)** metrics to understand how recently customers purchased, how frequently they purchase, and how much they spend.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of **sales performance, customer behavior, customer segments, category performance, and monthly trends**.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior using RFM analysis
- Segment customers based on their RFM scores
- Identify high-value and at-risk customer groups
- Track sales and customer trends over time
- Compare current-year performance with the previous year
- Provide an interactive dashboard for business analysis

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses a **Superstore sales dataset** containing information such as:

- Customer details
- Order and shipping information
- Order dates
- Sales and profit
- Products and categories
- Regions and locations

## 🔎 RFM Analysis

### Recency
Measures **how recently a customer made a purchase**.

### Frequency
Measures **how frequently a customer placed orders**.

### Monetary
Measures **how much a customer spent**.

Customers are scored on a **1–5 scale** and grouped into segments such as:

- Champions
- Loyal Customers
- Big Spenders
- At Risk
- Lost
- Others
